The Challenge

Many corporate websites settle for standard WordPress templates. St. Bernadine USA required a digital presence that matched their prestigious reputation—something fast, impenetrable, and deeply bespoke.

As the Lead Developer, I decisively bypassed WordPress. I built the platform from the ground up utilizing Custom HTML5, Vanilla JS, CSS3, and Node.js.
